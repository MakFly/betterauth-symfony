# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Symfony developers integrate a dependable authentication boundary into an
existing application. Maintainers need to verify that the public contracts,
documentation, demo, compatibility matrix, and release gates tell the same
truth. Both groups work in focused engineering contexts and value predictable,
copyable steps over product theatre.

## Product Purpose

BetterAuth Symfony provides encrypted PASETO access tokens and carefully bounded
application-owned authentication contracts. It helps teams add token security
without taking over their users, routes, persistence, or deployment choices.
Success means a developer can understand the ownership boundary, safely wire a
feature, and prove it locally before a maintainer releases it.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, reliable. The voice is direct, technical, and never theatrical.
Confidence comes from explicit contracts and visible evidence, not decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not imitate a fake terminal, a decorative SaaS dashboard, or an observability
screen made of hero metrics. Avoid purple gradients, glass surfaces, loud data
visualization, and UI that pretends to be an operations console rather than a
small registration product.

## Design Principles

- Make ownership visible: show what the application owns and what the bundle
  guarantees.
- Prefer proof over promise: states, responses, and safety properties must be
  inspectable.
- Keep the task in view: registration and account safety come before decoration.
- Use familiar technical affordances with restraint, inspired by Stripe,
  Symfony, and GitHub Settings.
- Let reliability feel quiet: use motion only to explain a state change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, reduced
motion, readable contrast, touch targets of at least 44px, adaptable light/dark
themes, safe-area spacing, and zoom without horizontal scrolling.
